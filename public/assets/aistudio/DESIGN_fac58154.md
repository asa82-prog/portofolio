# Design System: Khansa Shabihah Editorial Portfolio (Serene & Painterly Aesthetic)

## 1. Visual Identity & Brand Archetype
- **Brand Archetype**: The Thoughtful Craftsman / Editorial Sage (Literary, mindful, engineering precision).
- **Core Aesthetic**: *Aethera-inspired* editorial minimalism. Expansive whitespace, quiet serenity, high-contrast serif typography paired with warm, painterly natural landscapes.
- **Atmosphere**: Calm, serene, organic, literary, and structured.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Tokens

### Neutrals
- `--bg-canvas`: `#FFFFFF` — Primary stark white canvas providing maximum contrast and airy breathing room.
- `--bg-soft`: `#F7F8F6` — Subtle warm, organic off-white for cards, bento containers, and background modules.
- `--bg-card-tint`: `#FAF9F5` — Soft parchment paper tint for honors, recognition, and feature callouts.
- `--surface`: `#FFFFFF` — Elevated card surface.
- `--border-subtle`: `rgba(17, 19, 21, 0.08)` — Light hairline dividers and card outlines.
- `--border-default`: `rgba(17, 19, 21, 0.12)` — Standard card and pill button borders.

### Ink & Typography Colors
- `--ink-primary`: `#111315` — Deep charcoal/black ink for maximum legibility and bold headings.
- `--ink-secondary`: `#42474E` — Secondary text, subheadings, and metadata values.
- `--ink-muted`: `#686D76` — Captions, timestamps, descriptions, and section labels.

### Accents (Meadow & Forest sampled from Painterly Valley)
- `--accent-forest`: `#1E3A2F` — Deep evergreen moss foliage; primary brand accent for active states and badges.
- `--accent-forest-soft`: `#E8EFEA` — Soft muted sage/moss background wash for tags and badges.
- `--accent-meadow-green`: `#7BA05B` — Warm meadow leaf accent for subtle horizon lines and illustrations.
- `--accent-golden`: `#D4A359` — Golden sunlight / wildflower accent for delicate highlights.

---

## 3. Typography System

### Font Families
- **Display Serif**: `"Cormorant Garamond"`, Georgia, serif
  - *Weights*: 300 (Light), 400 (Regular), 600 (Semi-Bold), 700 (Bold), Italic
  - *Role*: Headings, editorial titles, quotes, project names, and literary highlights.
  - *Special Pattern*: Mixed-weight headline technique (e.g., `<span>` light weight 300 paired with `<strong>` bold weight 700).
- **Body & UI Sans**: `"Plus Jakarta Sans"`, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif
  - *Weights*: 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium), 600 (Semi-Bold)
  - *Role*: Body copy, navigation links, labels, pill buttons, metrics, and metadata.

### Type Scale
- **Display XXL**: `clamp(44px, 6.2vw, 82px)` / Line-height: `1.05` / Tracking: `-0.025em` (Hero headline)
- **Heading 1**: `clamp(34px, 4.2vw, 52px)` / Line-height: `1.15` / Tracking: `-0.02em` (Section titles)
- **Heading 2**: `clamp(26px, 3.2vw, 36px)` / Line-height: `1.25` / Tracking: `-0.015em` (Sub-sections, card titles)
- **Heading 3**: `20px` - `24px` / Line-height: `1.3` (Component headers)
- **Body Large**: `16px` / Line-height: `1.75` / Weight: `400`
- **Body Base**: `14.5px` - `15px` / Line-height: `1.65` / Weight: `400`
- **Section Label (Kicker)**: `11.5px` - `12px` / Weight: `600` / Uppercase / Letter-spacing: `0.14em`
- **Badge / Tag**: `12px` - `13px` / Weight: `500` / Letter-spacing: `0.01em`

---

## 4. Spacing, Grid & Layout
- **Max Width**: `1160px` for main content sections (`.sec`).
- **Section Padding**: `110px 6vw` (desktop), `80px 5vw` (tablet), `60px 4vw` (mobile).
- **Grid Layouts**:
  - Two-column asymmetric: `1.2fr | 0.8fr` (About & Education).
  - Two-column balanced: `1fr | 1fr` (Experience & Achievements, Featured Projects).
  - Three-column Bento: `repeat(3, 1fr)` (Skills & Capabilities).
- **Border Radius**:
  - `--radius-pill`: `9999px` (Buttons, tech chips, status badges)
  - `--radius-card`: `20px` - `24px` (Project cards, bento cards)
  - `--radius-container`: `28px` (Contact callout card)

---

## 5. UI Components & Patterns

### 1. Pill Buttons (`.btn-pill`)
- **Primary Pill**: Background `#111315`, text `#FFFFFF`, padding `13px 28px`, radius `9999px`. Hover: `translateY(-2px)`, background `#1E3A2F`.
- **Ghost Pill**: Background transparent, border `1px solid rgba(17,19,21,0.12)`, text `#111315`. Hover: border-color `#1E3A2F`, background `#F7F8F6`.

### 2. Navigation Chrome (Sticky Topbar)
- Height: `76px`, padding `0 5vw`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`, backdrop-filter `blur(12px)`.
- Left: Brand mark *"Khansa."* in Cormorant Garamond 700 (22px).
- Center: Minimalist links with muted ink `#686D76`, active/hover `#111315`.
- Right: "Get in Touch" primary pill CTA.

### 3. Cards & Bento Containers
- Soft tinted background (`#F7F8F6` or `#FAF9F5`), subtle 1px border (`rgba(17,19,21,0.08)`), generous inner padding (`28px` - `34px`).
- Subtle elevation on hover: `translateY(-4px)` to `translateY(-6px)`, box shadow `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(17,19,21,0.08)`.

### 4. Seamless Painterly Dissolve Mask
- Top gradient overlay: `linear-gradient(to bottom, #FFFFFF 0%, rgba(255,255,255,0.85) 35%, rgba(255,255,255,0) 100%)`.
- Blends pure editorial typography effortlessly into lush Ghibli-inspired valley watercolor artwork.

### 5. Motion & Transitions
- `--ease`: `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` (Smooth luxury editorial damping).
- Panning drift: Slow panoramic drift (`valleyDrift` 24s ease-in-out infinite alternate).
- Motion safety: Complete disablement with `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`.
